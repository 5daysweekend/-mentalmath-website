About the App

Mental Math is a playful and engaging brain trainer designed to improve your mental calculation skills. Train your abilities across various mathematical disciplines, track your speed, and beat your own high scores.

  • Diverse Exercises: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • Speed Training: Solve as many math problems as you can before the time runs out.
  • Secure Sign-In: Use your Google Account for a quick and secure authentication (OAuth 2.0). We do not store your personal profile data on external servers.

Privacy Policy

1. Privacy at a Glance

This privacy policy informs you about the nature, scope, and purpose of the processing of personal data within our application "Mental Math" and this website. We treat your data confidentially and in accordance with statutory data protection regulations (GDPR).

2. Data Processing via Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0)

Our application offers the option to authenticate using the Google Sign-In service. When you use this service, the application requests access to basic profile information from your Google Account (specifically your name and email address) via the official Google OAuth 2.0 procedure.

How we use this data: This data is processed strictly locally on your device to display your user profile within the app interface (e.g., showing your name in the main menu). We do not transfer, upload, or store this data on any external servers. We do not track, sell, or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to receive information about your stored personal data free of charge at any time. You also have the right to request the correction or erasure of this data. Since no user data is stored on our infrastructure, you can revoke the app's access at any time within your Google Account security settings.
